Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our digital bookstore, delivery formats, and future integrations.

What is PageRazor?

PageRazor is a modern digital bookstore dedicated to publishing and distributing concise, high-leverage ebooks and immersive audio editions. Our products focus on craft, career transitions, creator growth, and deep focus.

What products can I buy?

You can purchase digital ebooks (PDF and EPUB formats) and studio-recorded audio books (MP3 with chapter markers and action workbooks). In future phases, we will also offer bundle packages and companion digital toolkits.

Can I buy ebooks?

Yes. All ebooks are formatted for universal readability across Apple Books, Kindle, PDF readers, tablets, and smartphones.

Can I buy audio books?

Yes. Audio books are recorded in professional studios with dedicated narrators, crisp 320kbps audio quality, and include downloadable companion notes.

How does payment work?

In this Phase 1 frontend version, a simulated checkout demonstrates the ordering process. In Phase 5, PageRazor will integrate real Razorpay payments supporting UPI, Cards, NetBanking, and Wallets with server-side signature verification.

When will I receive my purchase?

Delivery is 100% digital and instantaneous. Once payment is confirmed, your products are automatically unlocked in your personal "My Library" dashboard.

Can I access my purchases later?

Yes. Your purchases are tied to your account with lifetime access. You can log in from any device to re-download your ebooks or stream your audio chapters.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. When Razorpay is connected in Phase 5, all transactions will be processed via PCI-DSS compliant 256-bit encrypted channels. Sensitive keys and card credentials are never stored on frontend servers.
